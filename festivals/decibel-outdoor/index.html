Privacy notice

Last updated: June 20, 2026

This is an unofficial fan-made project. Festival pages are not affiliated with or endorsed by their organizers.

Privacy contact

langenn@proton.me

Data we process

  • Your email address, account ID and authentication information.
  • Your display name and optional profile picture.
  • The sets you select or mark as important.
  • Your party membership and the timetable selections shared with party members.
  • Technical information required by Supabase to provide authentication, database and file-storage services.

Passwords are handled by Supabase Authentication and are not visible to the publisher.

Why we use this data

We process this data to provide accounts, synchronize your timetable between devices, display party selections and keep the service secure. The legal basis is providing the service you request and our legitimate interest in operating and securing the app.

Storage and sharing

Account, profile, timetable and party data are stored using Supabase. Profile pictures are stored in Supabase Storage. Party members can see your display name, profile picture and the sets you selected while you share a party.

More information: Supabase Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Retention

Local timetable selections remain on your device until you clear browser data. Account data is retained while your account is active or as long as needed to provide the service.

You can permanently delete your account through Account -> Delete account. This removes your account, profile, synchronized timetable data, party membership and stored profile picture. This action cannot be undone.

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data through the privacy contact above. You may also object to processing or submit a complaint to your national data-protection authority.

Local storage and third-party services

The app uses browser local storage for functional preferences and login-related state. It does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Google Fonts and Supabase may receive technical connection data such as your IP address when their services are loaded.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the app or its data processing changes. The latest version will remain available through the Privacy button.
